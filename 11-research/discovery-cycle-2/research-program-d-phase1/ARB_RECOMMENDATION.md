# ARB Recommendation
## Discovery Cycle 2 Program D Phase 1

- **Outcome:** REQUIRES REVISION
- **Decision:** REVISE_MODEL
- **Rationale:** Elevated false-transition rate indicates over-sensitive trigger assumptions.; Missed-transition rate indicates incomplete transition mechanism coverage.; Confidence calibration is weak under transition-risk scoring.; Robustness under stress/event subsets is weaker than at least one simpler baseline.; Transition detection accuracy is not superior to simpler baselines.
