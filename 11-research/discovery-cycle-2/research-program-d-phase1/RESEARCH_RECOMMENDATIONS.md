# Research Recommendations
## Discovery Cycle 2 Program D Phase 1

- **Outcome:** REQUIRES REVISION
- **Retain as governing model:** False

### Revision Priorities
- Elevated false-transition rate indicates over-sensitive trigger assumptions.
- Missed-transition rate indicates incomplete transition mechanism coverage.
- Confidence calibration is weak under transition-risk scoring.
- Robustness under stress/event subsets is weaker than at least one simpler baseline.
- Transition detection accuracy is not superior to simpler baselines.

### ARB Recommendation
Transition Engine v1 should be revised before remaining the governing explanatory model.
