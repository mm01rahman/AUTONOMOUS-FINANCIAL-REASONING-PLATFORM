## Campaign governance standard

Every campaign must define:

1. Research question
2. Economic rationale
3. Supporting literature
4. Datasets
5. Hypotheses
6. Expected mechanisms
7. Experiment design
8. Validation methodology
9. Acceptance criteria
10. Failure criteria
11. Statistical tests
12. Required evidence
13. Expected deliverables

## Validation floor

No candidate advances without the full validation stack:

- walk-forward validation
- combinatorial purged cross validation
- Monte Carlo
- stress testing
- sensitivity analysis
- deflated Sharpe ratio
- probabilistic Sharpe ratio
- White's Reality Check where applicable
- probability of backtest overfitting where applicable

## First completed campaign

The first completed Phase G campaign is **Macro Alpha — Baseline Audit** under `11-research/phase-g/macro-alpha/`. It formalizes the prior Phase E macro-only evidence inside IKROS and concludes that the baseline macro-only candidate is **not** eligible for advancement.
