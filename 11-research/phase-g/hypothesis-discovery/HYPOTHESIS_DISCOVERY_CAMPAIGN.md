# Campaign 0004 - Institutional Alpha Hypothesis Discovery

## Research mission

Discover economically explainable alpha hypotheses using only the approved
Institutional Six-State Overlay Taxonomy v1, the approved Institutional
Regime-Conditioned Feature Catalogue v1, governed AFRP datasets, and existing
IKROS institutional knowledge.

## Primary research question

Which combinations of approved features form economically plausible,
statistically testable, regime-conditioned alpha hypotheses?

## Inputs

- Institutional Six-State Overlay Taxonomy v1
- Institutional Regime-Conditioned Feature Catalogue v1
- Governed AFRP XAU/USD datasets
- Existing literature and historical analogues already curated in IKROS
- Previously rejected hypotheses and contradiction records

## Scope

This campaign generates and prioritizes hypotheses only.

It does **not** validate, backtest, optimize, or parameter-tune any candidate.

## Hypothesis generation standard

Every candidate must specify:

1. Research question
2. Economic theory
3. Feature set
4. Regime scope
5. Expected direction
6. Expected horizon
7. Expected holding period
8. Validation plan
9. Acceptance criteria
10. Rejection criteria
11. Required evidence
12. Confidence prior

## Scientific review dimensions

- Economic plausibility
- Consistency with literature
- Consistency with previous AFRP findings
- Novelty
- Expected information gain
- Potential alpha capacity
- Explainability
- Institutional usefulness

## Deliverables

- Institutional Alpha Hypothesis Catalogue v1
- Hypothesis Cards
- Economic Mechanism Atlas
- Hypothesis Dependency Graph
- Research Priority Matrix
- Expected Validation Matrix
- Final Campaign Report
- ARB Recommendation
