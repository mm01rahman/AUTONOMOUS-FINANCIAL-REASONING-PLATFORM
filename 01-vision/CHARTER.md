# AFRP Charter — Vision & Product Mission

> Derived verbatim in intent from `00-governance/000_ENGINEERING_CONSTITUTION.md` §1.

## Mission

Build an AI-native, autonomous financial reasoning and execution system for complex,
high-volatility instruments (headlined by XAU/USD spot gold) that rejects
point-estimate price prediction in favor of:

- formal epistemic belief formation (DSmT over Dedekind's lattice),
- multi-agent structural reasoning (six domain agents),
- mathematical evidence fusion (PCR5),
- automated risk governance (policy projection with a No-Trade default).

## Product Objectives

1. **Engineering Operating System (EOS)** — a self-describing, self-verifying
   execution governor: every change is a Work Package with bounded files, quality
   gates, and machine-verifiable evidence.
2. **Runtime Platform** — six cognitive layers connected exclusively through
   versioned Protobuf contracts, deterministic under seed 42, degrading gracefully
   under telemetry loss, and always preferring No Trade over a Poor Trade.
3. **Research Platform** — offline strategy discovery and calibration that informs
   the runtime without corrupting its safety invariants.

## Non-Goals

- Point-estimate price forecasting as a product.
- Discretionary/manual trade decisioning inside the runtime path.
- Any evolution channel other than Requirements → Evidence → ADRs → Review Gates.
